PoR / Progression or Regression By The Data Dugout Menu Home The Article Methodology Results About About Why this exists PoR started as a spreadsheet question: how much of a player's stat line is the player, and how much is the ball finding grass? Four seasons of testing later, it's a metric with a track record — built in the open, tested against real outcomes, and revised whenever the data pushed back on an assumption. This site exists to lay out the method plainly: what the score measures, how it was validated, where it holds up, and where it doesn't. Corrections and edge cases are part of the process, not a footnote — if a number here changes, it's because the testing got more rigorous, not less. Questions, pushback, and better ideas are welcome.
