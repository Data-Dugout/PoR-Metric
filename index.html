A Sabermetric Hitting Metric

Some hitters aren't as good — or as bad — as their stat line says.

PoR (Progression or Regression) measures how much of a hitter's season was skill versus batted-ball luck — and uses that gap to flag who's likely to swing the other way next year.

Why it exists

Batting average lies to you at least once a season.

A hitter can scorch the ball all year and have nothing to show for it, or get bloop singles all August and look like a star. PoR isolates that gap — actual outcomes minus expected outcomes on contact — and turns it into a single 0–100 score.
