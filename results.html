All figures use the four-input PoR score (BA, SLG, wOBA, BABIP). Why it exists Batting average lies to you at least once a season. A hitter can scorch the ball all year and have nothing to show for it, or get bloop singles all August and look like a star. PoR isolates that gap — actual outcomes minus expected outcomes on contact — and turns it into a single 0–100 score. 1,423 qualified player-seasons tested, 2021–2024 4 inputs: BA, SLG, wOBA, and BABIP vs. expected 94.3% accuracy at the tightest confidence threshold (500+ PA, wRC+ 90/110 split) Progression candidates Below-average hitters (wRC+ < 100) who got unlucky — high PoR score, low actual production.
